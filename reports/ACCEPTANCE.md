Found by these tests and fixed before the release: a model that is a single shape was written into the scene file a second time on every save, in rc4 too (a 105 MB scene that took 20 s to open is now 24 KB and opens in 0.6 s); the joints of a layered material were not found on normal maps the graphics card keeps packed small, as the built program's presets are (the first lab runs read the source's unpacked maps and passed); and an export filter added for the clouds' noise had packed the geometry of every library model a second time, 96 MB, seen when the build was measured.

And one found by the last run of all, the end-to-end test from what was to be the final AppImage: its *verify* launch ran out of the build machine's memory, twice. Every plant material that sways in the wind had a shader of its own (in software rendering, where a shader is compiled for the processor, the two in the test scene cost 224 MiB; with a graphics card far less, but a wood of thirty kinds of tree would have had sixty), and neither they nor the copies of meshes that scatters are drawn from were ever let go: each scene opened in a session left its own behind. Twins of one kind now share one shader and go when their scene does (module suite, 7 more checks). The AppImage was built again, and everything in this report was run again from it: the lab checks, the screenshot tour and the six launches.
