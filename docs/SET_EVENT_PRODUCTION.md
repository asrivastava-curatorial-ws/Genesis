# SET: event production

Parametric set pieces, edited in the Properties panel:

- **Stage decks** from 2 m × 1 m modules on legs.
- **Truss**: box, triangle, ladder; bend into arcs and circles.
- **LED walls** from real cabinet specifications, straight or curved, flown or stacked, with a report of size, pixels, pitch, cabinets, weight, power (maximum and typical, amps at 230 V and 120 V), data ports and viewing distance, and a pixel map.
- **Flown line arrays** with realistic splay; a **flying screen** under a helicopter.
- **Fixtures**: moving heads that pan and tilt, washes, downlights, bulbs, IES photometry, area lights; beams show in stage haze.
- **Crowds** of thousands at a set density: concert, seated, milling, a frustrated queue.
- **Effects**: fire, smoke, fountains, falling leaves, confetti, CO2 jets, cold sparks, flame projectors.

> **All weight, power, rigging and data figures are planning estimates** from the panel or fixture specification. They are not certified structural, rigging or electrical approvals; a qualified engineer must check any real rig.
