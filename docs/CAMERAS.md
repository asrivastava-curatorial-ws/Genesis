# Cameras

- **Save the view** as a named camera (CAMERAS section, or Add > Camera From This View, Ctrl+Alt+C).
- **Look through** a saved camera: select it and press Numpad 0 (View > Look Through Selected Camera). In the standalone program the main view really moves to it.
- Lens (focal length), near clip (cut the view open).
- **Plans, sections and elevations** in one click: orthographic cameras whose near clip is the cutting plane.
- **LOOK** section: per-camera finish: exposure, colour, warmth, depth of field, two-point perspective, vignette, grain, pencil sketch, and 14 built-in looks (Natural, Crisp daylight, Warm afternoon, Cool morning, Moody, Cinematic, Black and white, Pencil sketch, Watercolour, Painting, Outlined, Manga, Clay model, Golden glow), plus your own, saved in the project.
